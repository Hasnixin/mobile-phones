Mobiles Phone's

Mobiles Phone's have become an essential part of our daily lives. They are not only used for communication but also for accessing the internet, taking photos,online learning, entertainment, and managing work. Modern smartphones are like mini-computers that keep us connected to the world anytime and anywhere.

Advantage's and Disadvantage's of mobile's

Advantages

  • Instant communication anywhere, anytime
  • Access to infromation online learning
  • Entertainment(music, videos , games)
  • Emergency use and safety

Disadvantages

  • Mobile phone addiction
  • Less face-toface communication
  • Cyberbullying and privacy risks
  • Distraction from studies or work
